# Reference inputs needed for exact matching

The compiled roleplay build uses the saved public references. Those references mix promotional framing, account types, publication dates and devices; they cannot establish complete current applications. Functional tests, shared package hashes and nonblank startup captures do not certify 1:1 appearance.

For each required style, identify the app version/build, country, iPhone model, iOS version, appearance and account/product type. An unaltered recording covering Home → selected transaction → review → result → history supplies the missing transitions and screen geometry. Shopify also needs the selected Products/Menu states. Delivered notification examples establish their title/body, installed identity and sound.

The current app supports local fictional transactions; it does not reproduce account enrollment, banking authorization, exchange networking or every product route. In particular, Coinbase Card/Rewards/separate Wallet/Advanced trade, Polymarket combos/chat/livestreams, full credit/investment bank products, and exact wallet collectible artwork remain outside the verified matching coverage.

[Visual coverage](VISUAL-COVERAGE.md) identifies the pictured and unpictured states for all 20 styles. [Verification status](STATUS.md) separates executed checks from remaining comparisons. Selecting those target states makes the remaining 1:1 goal concrete and testable.
